1. Summary

  • The Game runs entirely on your device. We do not operate accounts, servers, or tracking in this build.
  • We do not collect, sell, or share personal data.
  • Your game progress is stored only in your browser/device local storage.
  • In-app purchases in this build are simulated and process no payment or payment data.

2. Information we process

Local game data. The Game stores gameplay state (coins, lives, level, boosters) in your device's local storage under the key candyblast. This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us, and is not personal data.

No identifiers or analytics. This build sets no cookies, contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not fingerprint your device. Because we use no non-essential storage or trackers, no consent banner is required.

3. Children's privacy

The Game is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your jurisdiction). Because no personal data is collected, the Game is designed to align with COPPA and the UK/EU "age-appropriate design" expectations. If real accounts, ads, or analytics are added in future, this policy will be updated and appropriate parental-consent mechanisms added.

4. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA-CPRA)

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on our side. You can erase all locally stored game data at any time using Settings → Reset game data in the Game, or by clearing your browser/app storage. We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA.

5. Data security & retention

Game data is retained locally until you clear it. As no data is transmitted or stored by us, there is no server-side retention. If a future build introduces accounts, we will add transport encryption, server-side controls, and defined retention periods.

6. Payments (future)

Purchases in this build are simulated and collect no payment information. In production, payments would be processed by Stripe, Google Play Billing, or Apple In-App Purchase, each of which acts as an independent controller/processor of payment data under their own privacy policies. We would never receive or store full card numbers.

7. Changes

We may update this policy as the Game evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
